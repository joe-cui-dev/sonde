# Sonde

Sonde researches a question using retrieved source evidence and produces a cited report.

## Language

**Synthesis**:
The stage that turns research notes and source evidence into a research report.

**Report preview**:
An incomplete view of a report while synthesis is in progress. Its citations have not yet undergone final validation.

**Final report**:
The completed research report after structural and citation validation. Citation validation may remove unsupported references or produce warnings; it does not establish that every claim is true.
